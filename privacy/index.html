Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 August 2026

Meno is a journaling and habit-tracking app. This policy explains what information the app collects, why it is collected, and what you can do about it. It is written to be read, not to be skimmed past.

Meno is developed and operated by an individual developer based in Jordan. Throughout this policy, "we" and "us" refer to that developer, and "you" refers to the person using the app.

Information we collect

You give us the following when you create an account and use the app:

  • Account details — your email address, username, and full name.
  • Profile details — your country or location, if you choose to add one, and your profile photo, if you upload one.
  • Content you create — journal notes, habits, daily feelings, and any text you type into the app.
  • Progress data — streaks, levels, coins earned, and skins you own or have equipped.
  • Subscription status — whether you hold an active Pro subscription and when it expires.

We do not collect your contacts, your precise GPS location, your health data, or anything from other apps on your device. We do not use advertising identifiers.

What we do not use

Meno contains no third-party analytics, no advertising networks, and no tracking software. We do not build advertising profiles, and we do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with data brokers or advertisers. There is no exception to this.

Why we collect it

  • To create and maintain your account and sign you in.
  • To store and display the notes, habits, and progress you create, and sync them across your devices.
  • To calculate streaks, levels, and coins.
  • To determine whether Pro features are available to you.
  • To respond when you contact us for support.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, our legal basis for this processing is the performance of our contract with you — we cannot provide the app without it — and, where relevant, your consent, which you may withdraw at any time by deleting your account.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on servers operated by Supabase, located in Ireland (EU West). Supabase acts as our data processor. Profile photos and skin assets are held in Supabase Storage in the same region.

Transactional emails, including sign-in codes, are delivered by Resend. Resend receives only your email address and the contents of the message sent to you.

If you access the app from outside the European Economic Area, your data is transferred to and stored in Ireland.

Payments

All purchases inside Meno are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive or store your card number, billing address, or any other payment details. We receive only a confirmation from Apple that a subscription is active and when it expires.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account from within the app, your account and its associated content are removed from our database. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Backups may retain copies for a short period before being overwritten in the normal course of operation.

Your rights

You can, at any time:

  • Access your data — most of it is visible directly in the app.
  • Correct your name, username, location, and profile photo in Settings.
  • Delete your account and content, from Settings inside the app.
  • Request a copy of your data, or ask us to restrict or object to processing, by emailing us.

If you are in the EEA or UK and believe we have handled your data improperly, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

Children

Meno is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Security

Accounts are protected by one-time codes sent to your email rather than passwords stored in the app. Data is encrypted in transit. Access to the database is restricted by row-level security rules, so your content is readable only by your own account.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we will notify affected users without undue delay if a breach affects your personal data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that materially affects you, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you inside the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests concerning your data, can be sent to support@meno.fyi. We aim to respond within 30 days.
