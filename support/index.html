Meno Support

Need help with Meno? Email us at support@meno.fyi and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

How do I cancel Meno Pro?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Meno.

How do I delete my account?

In Meno, go to Profile, open Settings, and tap Delete Account.

Found a bug?

In Meno, go to Profile, open Settings, and tap Report a Bug, or email us directly.
